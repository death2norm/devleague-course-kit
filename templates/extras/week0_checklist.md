# DevLeague {{COURSE_NAME}} · Week 0 Checklist

*Finish everything here before the Week 0 orientation if you can. Anything that doesn't work, bring to orientation and we'll fix it there. About 60–90 minutes in total.*

## 1. Course accounts (15 minutes)

- [ ] **Moodle:** sign in at learn.devleague.com with the details in your welcome email. Open the course and find the Start Here section.
- [ ] **Slack:** accept the Slack invite and join the course channel. Post a one-line hello: your name, where you're joining from, and what you do now.
- [ ] **Zoom:** install or update the Zoom app (breakout rooms work best in the app). Test your microphone, speakers and camera at zoom.us/test.
- [ ] **Read the Student Handbook** (Start Here). It explains the schedule, the voucher rule (77 of 90 on a practice exam) and how to get help.

## 2. Cisco Packet Tracer (30–40 minutes)

Packet Tracer is Cisco's free network simulator. We use it in six sessions. Follow the Packet Tracer Quick Start in Start Here; it has screenshots for each step.

- [ ] **Create a free Cisco Networking Academy account** at netacad.com. Use an email you check; you'll need it to sign in to Packet Tracer.
- [ ] **Download Packet Tracer** from the Resource Hub page on netacad.com, under Learning Resources (sign in first), for Windows, macOS or Ubuntu. Use the newest version listed.
- [ ] **Install it and open it.** When it asks, click the green **Skills For All** button and sign in with your Networking Academy account.
- [ ] **Open the test file** {{PREFIX}}_W0_TestFile.pkt (Start Here in Moodle). Wait until the link lights turn green, then ping from PC-A to PC-B (steps in the Quick Start). Take a screenshot of the successful ping.

## 3. TryHackMe (10 minutes)

- [ ] **Accept your TryHackMe invite.** It comes by email from DevLeague or TryHackMe within a few days of enrollment; check your spam folder too.
- [ ] **Sign in and check that your account shows Premium.** Follow the TryHackMe Quick Start in Start Here.
- [ ] **Open the room "What is Networking?"** and join it. That's your first lab in Session 1.

## 4. Device check (5 minutes)

- [ ] Your computer is a Windows 11 or Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 PC with an Intel or AMD processor, or a Mac with macOS 12 or newer (Apple-silicon Macs run Packet Tracer through Rosetta 2, which macOS offers to install). A Chromebook, tablet or phone can't run Packet Tracer.
- [ ] You can have Zoom, a browser and Packet Tracer open at the same time without slowdowns (about 8 GB of memory or more works well).
- [ ] Your work laptop may block lab sites or software installs. If it does, use a personal computer.
- [ ] A wired connection or a strong Wi-Fi signal where you'll sit for class.

## 5. Warm-up (10 minutes)

- [ ] **Take the Week 0 Warm-Up quiz** in Moodle: 10 questions on binary numbers and subnet basics. It doesn't count toward anything. If it feels hard, that's normal. Session 3 teaches all of it, and your score just tells us where to spend extra time.

## 6. At the Week 0 orientation

- [ ] Join on time with your camera on, from the Zoom app.
- [ ] Have Packet Tracer open with the test file loaded, and TryHackMe signed in.
- [ ] Bring any step above that didn't work. We'll fix it together.

## Something didn't work?

Ask in the course Slack channel (your TA answers within 24 hours) or email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Please don't wait until Session 1.
