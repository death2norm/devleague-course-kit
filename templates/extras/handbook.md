## What This Course Is

This course prepares you to pass CompTIA {{COURSE_NAME}} {{EXAM_CODE}} on your first attempt. It's taught live: a 1-hour Week 0 orientation, then 18 three-hour evening sessions over six weeks. Each session explains exam objectives through workplace stories, shows you the trap answer the exam uses, and works practice questions out loud. Labs happen in class, in small breakout groups, in Cisco Packet Tracer and TryHackMe.

The course includes your CompTIA exam voucher and a retake. You earn the voucher by scoring 77 of 90 on a full-length practice exam, as this handbook explains.

## How Each Session Works

1. **Lecture blocks.** Each block covers part of an exam objective: a workplace story, the concept, the trap the exam uses, and a practice question you answer in the chat.
2. **Lab.** You work through a Packet Tracer file, a TryHackMe room or a worksheet with your breakout group (the same group every night) and complete a short worksheet.
3. **Debrief.** The class reviews what the lab showed.
4. **After class.** Take the nightly quiz in Moodle (10 or 12 questions; from Session 4 on, plus 2 subnetting questions) and answer the three short reflection questions. Try to finish within 24 hours while it's fresh. The quiz allows unlimited attempts and explains every answer.

At the end of each week (Sessions 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15), a 15-question **Mixed Review** brings back earlier sessions, and after Session 9 a 45-question **Week 3 Checkpoint** gives you a halfway read. They're optional and don't count toward anything.

## Attendance, Recordings and Make-Up

- **Come to every session live when you can.** The chat questions and breakout labs are where most of the learning happens.
- **Sessions are recorded** and posted in Moodle, normally by the next day.
- **If you miss a session,** make it up within 7 days: watch the recording, submit that session's lab worksheet, and score 80% or higher on its nightly quiz. Your TA checks make-ups and sends one reminder in Slack if one is missing.
- Attendance doesn't decide your voucher. Your practice exam score does. But students who skip sessions rarely reach 77 of 90, and make-ups are how we know you're keeping up.

## Practice Exams and Your Voucher

Every practice exam has 90 questions in 90 minutes, weighted by domain like the real exam. Each exam draws from its own sealed question pool, so you don't see the same question twice across exams.

| Exam | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Practice Exam A** | At home, opening at least 48 hours before Session 17 and closing before Session 18 | A diagnostic. Your score by domain shapes the final review in Session 17. |
| **Practice Exam B** | Live in Session 18, cameras on, one attempt | The readiness exam. **77 of 90 or higher earns your voucher.** |
| **Practice Exam C** | At home, within two weeks after your study plan arrives | A second chance at 77 of 90, with new questions |
| **Practice Exam D** | At home, 2–3 weeks after Exam C closes | A third chance, with new questions, after you retake your two weakest domains' quizzes and talk with your TA |

**77 of 90 (about 85%) is the only requirement for the voucher.** The program team emails your voucher code within 3 business days of a qualifying score (for Exam C or D, after any score-check call).

### If you score below 77 of 90 on Exam B

- Within two business days, you get a personal study plan built from your results by domain.
- Practice Exam C opens for two weeks. You take it at home: one 90-minute attempt, closed book, after agreeing to an honor statement.
- 77 of 90 or higher on Exam C earns your voucher.

### If you score below 77 of 90 on Exam C

- Retake the nightly quizzes for your two weakest domains until you score 80% or higher on each.
- Have a 15-minute call with your TA.
- Practice Exam D then opens for two weeks, with new questions and the same at-home rules.
- 77 of 90 or higher on Exam D earns your voucher.

If Exam D doesn't go your way either, you get 3 more months of access to the recordings and practice exams, plus a free seat in the next cohort's Sessions 17 and 18 and that cohort's live Exam B.

### If you miss Exam B

Tell the program team within 48 hours and you can take a makeup Exam B at home within 7 days. If you don't contact us within 48 hours, you move to the Exam C path.

### If your camera fails during Exam B

Keep working. Your TA will move you to a breakout room and try for 5 minutes to get the camera going. If it can't be fixed, your attempt counts under the at-home rules (honor statement and score check). If you lose more than 10 minutes of exam time, you take the makeup Exam B at home within 7 days instead. If you lose less than that, keep going; no extra time is added.

### Score checks

If your score on an at-home exam is more than 20 percentage points above your previous practice exam, the program team sets up a short, friendly call about how you prepared before releasing the voucher. It's a conversation, not an accusation. The same rule applies to everyone.

## Academic Integrity

- Practice exams are **closed book**: no notes, other people, other tabs, websites or AI tools.
- At-home exams start with an **honor statement** you agree to before the first question.
- Don't copy, photograph or share practice exam questions. The pools are sealed so every future student gets a fair test, and the questions only predict your real score if you haven't seen them before.
- Nightly quizzes, Mixed Reviews and the PBQ practice are for learning. Use your notes and the recordings if you like. They don't count toward the voucher.
- Breaking these rules can cost you the voucher. We'd much rather you ask for help.

## The Real Exam

- **The exam:** up to 90 questions in 90 minutes, multiple choice and performance-based. The passing score is 720 on a 100–900 scale.
- **Schedule it soon.** Most students do best testing within two to three weeks of earning the voucher.
- **Vouchers expire.** Each voucher has a fixed expiration date, which is in your voucher email. It can't be extended, so schedule before it expires.
- **ID:** you need a valid government photo ID that exactly matches the name on your CompTIA account, for online and test-center exams.
- **If you don't pass the first time:** your course includes a retake. Under CompTIA's current policy, there's no wait before a second attempt; after that, you wait 14 days before each further attempt.
- **If you don't pass on the retake either:** you get 3 more months of access to the recordings and practice exams, plus a reduced-price voucher for another attempt.
- **When you pass,** tell us. Your certification is valid for three years and renews through continuing education.
