# TryHackMe Quick Start

*For DevLeague {{COURSE_NAME}} students · Read this once before the Week 0 orientation · About 10 minutes*

## What TryHackMe Is

TryHackMe is the lab site we use in the sessions that aren't in Packet Tracer. Each lab gives you a **room**, which is a guided lesson broken into numbered **tasks**. DevLeague has created a Premium account for you, so every room in this course is unlocked through 30 days after Session 18.

## 1. Sign In for the First Time

1. Watch for an email with your TryHackMe invite. It comes from DevLeague or TryHackMe within a few days of enrollment, so check your spam folder too.
2. Follow the link in the email and set your password.
3. Go to **tryhackme.com** and click **Log In** in the top-right corner.
4. Check that your account shows **Premium**. If it doesn't, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} before the Week 0 orientation.

## 2. Open a Room

1. Every session's Lab page in Moodle has a direct link to that night's room. Click it, or paste it into your browser.
2. If you see a **Join Room** button, click it. That saves your progress to your account.
3. The room opens to a list of tasks. **Only do the tasks your Moodle Lab page lists.** Other tasks are optional extra practice.
4. Try it now with your first lab: the room **What is Networking?**

![A room page: click Join Room first](shots/thm_02_room.png){width=5.5}

![The room's numbered tasks; click one to open it](shots/thm_02b_tasks.png){width=5.5}

## 3. Work Through a Task

1. Click a task's title to open it.
2. Read the lesson. Questions appear at the bottom of the task.
3. Type your answer in the box and click **Check**. A correct answer turns green.
4. If you're stuck, ask your breakout group first, then click the light-bulb **hint** button if the question has one.

![A question at the bottom of a task: type the answer, then Check](shots/thm_03_task.png){width=5.5}

## 4. Practice Machines (Some Labs Only)

Some rooms ask you to start a machine. Your Lab page tells you when.

- **Start Machine** turns on the computer the lesson is about.
- **Start AttackBox** opens a Linux workstation inside your browser, with the tools already installed.

Machines take 1–3 minutes to boot, so start them as soon as you open the room. When you're done, click **Terminate** to shut them down.

## 5. Take Your Screenshot

Every TryHackMe lab asks for a screenshot that shows the room name and your completed tasks (the green checkmarks or the progress bar).

- **Windows:** press **Windows + Shift + S**, then drag a box around the area you want.
- **Mac:** press **Shift + Command + 4**, then drag a box around the area you want.

Windows copies the image to the clipboard (paste it into Paint and save it, or click the notification to save it); a Mac saves it to the desktop.

## 6. Submit in Moodle

1. Open the session's **Lab** page in Moodle.
2. Click **Add submission**.
3. Drag in your screenshot and your completed worksheet.
4. Click **Save changes**. The status should say **Submitted for grading**.

![The Moodle lab page with the Add submission button](shots/thm_04_moodle.png){width=5.5}

## 7. Breakout Rooms During the Lab

- After the second break, Zoom moves you into your breakout room. Accept the prompt if one appears.
- You'll have the same group every session. Do Part 1 on your own, then work Part 2 out loud together.
- Click **Ask for Help** in Zoom to bring the instructor or TA into your room.
- When time's up, Zoom brings everyone back to the main room for the debrief.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| I can't log in | Use **Forgot password** on the login page. If that doesn't work, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. |
| A room says Premium is required | You may be signed in to a personal account. Log out and sign in with your DevLeague account. |
| The machine won't start or hangs | Wait 2 minutes, refresh the page, then click **Terminate** and start it again. |
| A page won't load on my work laptop | Work networks often block lab sites. Use a personal computer or home network. |
| The browser acts strangely | Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and close extra tabs. |
