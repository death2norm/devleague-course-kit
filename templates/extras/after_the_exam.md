## When You Finish

- The exam is up to **90 questions in 90 minutes**. The passing score is **720 on a scale of 100–900**. It's a scaled score, not a percentage.
- You'll see your result on the screen when you finish. Your **score report** shows the objectives where you lost points. Save a copy either way.
- **Tell the program team** your result at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}, pass or not, and share your score report if you're willing. It's how we improve the course.

## Keeping It Active

- {{COURSE_NAME}} is **valid for three years** from your passing date.
- **Renew with continuing education:** currently **30 continuing education units (CEUs)** over the three years, plus a CE fee for the three-year cycle (you can pay it all at once before your expiration date). Training, conferences, college courses and documented work experience can count. Check CompTIA's continuing education pages for the current rules and fee.
- **Or renew by passing a higher-level certification.** Earning Security+ inside your three years also renews {{COURSE_NAME}}. Check CompTIA's current list before you count on it.
- **Log CEUs as you earn them,** not in the last month. A lapsed certification means taking the exam again.

## If It Didn't Go Your Way

- **Your voucher includes one retake.** Tell the program team, and use your score report to pick the two domains to study first.
- Under CompTIA's current policy, there's **no wait before a second attempt**, then **14 days** before a third or later attempt. Check the policy when you book.
- Before you rebook: rewatch the sessions for the two weakest domains on your score report, retake those nightly quizzes until you can say why every wrong answer is wrong (aim for 90%), and reread the Trap Atlas and domain cheat sheets for those domains.
- **If the retake doesn't pass either:** you get 3 more months of access to the recordings and practice exams, plus a reduced-price voucher for another attempt. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
